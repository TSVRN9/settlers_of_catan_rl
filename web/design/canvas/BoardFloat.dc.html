Play Watch Results About SETTLERS
You rolled an 8
Both eights rise off the table: a wood to you from the settlement on the 8 and the 3, two to Red's city on the other. The pieces that collected rise with their tiles.
Tiles that move
Every tile hovers six pixels above the ground with a soft shadow. A roll lifts the tiles that pay, for about a second, then they settle. Corner pieces take the height of the tallest tile they touch, so a settlement on a rolled corner is carried up with it.
The same motion, other meanings
Hover a candidate future and the tiles that differ lift. Turn on the yield lens and height becomes the chance a tile pays. Scrub the analyser and the tiles the net looked at come up. One verb, learned once.
Against the columns
Less monumental at rest, since the floating board is nearly the flat board with shadows. It earns its keep in motion. Reduced-motion users get the shadow change without the travel.
