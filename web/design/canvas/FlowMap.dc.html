Four things this changes
Nobody configures a game they have not seen

A board is dealt on arrival and the first move is there waiting. Seat and opponents change in place, mid-game if you like, and the seed is a field you can paste into.

The opening is taught, not assumed

Placement decides more than any later move and is the one decision the net can explain without a hand to reason about. It deserves its own screen, with the corners scored.

Interrupts take the screen

A seven is three decisions in a row and a trade is a negotiation with three opponents. Each gets one surface that finishes what it started, instead of a stack of dialogs.

A finished game is the way into the analysis

Play and Watch are strangers today. The result should hand the game to the analyser, opened at the move that cost you most — which is the only reason most people would ever open it.
