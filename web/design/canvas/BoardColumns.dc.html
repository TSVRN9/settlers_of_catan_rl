Turn 31 — your move
The island as a core sample: nineteen columns standing in dark space, the gaps between them going all the way down.
One angle, chosen once
The tilt is fixed at about 44 degrees so the top faces stay readable and pieces can stand upright on them as elevations, which is what they already are. The camera never moves: this is a projection, not a 3D scene.
What the depth is for
Nothing yet. The columns are all one height. Every piece sits at a corner shared by three tiles, so the moment column heights carry data the pieces have nowhere to stand. This is a look, and a strong one; the floating tiles next door are the version that can also work as an instrument.
Cost
The board is 15 percent shorter on screen and the two faces per column add 38 polygons. Hit targets stay on the top faces.
