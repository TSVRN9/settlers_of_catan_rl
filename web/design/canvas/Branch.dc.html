What rewinding is
Press the left arrow during your own game and the board steps back. Any move you make from a past step starts a branch. The bots play the branch fresh; the dice are re-rolled, so a branch is a new game that shares an opening, not a replay with one change.
What happens to it
A branch you keep becomes the game. A branch you leave stays on the curve as a faint line, and the result screen shows every road not taken next to the one you took.
